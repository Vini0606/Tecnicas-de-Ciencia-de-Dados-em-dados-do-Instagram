\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
This work developed and internally validated, by incremental information over raw metrics, a hybrid text-mining methodology that serves as the engine of a decision-support tool for political communication teams on Instagram. The approach integrates sentiment analysis (XLM-RoBERTa, validated and fine-tuned on annotated comments from the same dataset), topic modeling (BERTopic, with labels refined by a large language model) and unsupervised learning (Principal Component Analysis followed by an automatic search over clustering algorithms and hyperparameters), and organizes the results into an engagement funnel that combines the activity levels of the Consumers' Online Brand-Related Activities (COBRAs) framework with the stages of growth marketing. The methodology separates the communication team's discourse, in captions, from the audience's reaction, in comments, and was applied to a 71-day collection (16 August to 25 October 2026) of the profiles of the 26 Brazilian state governors with an active account, comprising 8,488 posts, 75,634 comments and 16,824 third-party mentions, organized in a layered, versioned data architecture. The central finding is that the volume of a reaction does not indicate its direction: in the feed, posts above the body have 16.6\% negative comments, against 9.0\% in the body, a difference that holds when any single profile is removed and that appears in 17 of the 19 comparable profiles. In Reels, the negativity of viral videos (28.1\%, against 10.1\% in the body) is concentrated in a single profile and does not generalize. To turn this diagnosis into decisions, the work proposed a North Star Metric of qualified positive engagement, which reordered 19 of the 24 profiles with sufficient data relative to raw engagement; an ICE score to prioritize agendas and themes; and a composite score per profile, decomposable into five dimensions, whose Consistency dimension is estimated by a regression over the profile's own posts, with a confidence interval and a Trend state. A case study of three profiles selected by rule and an interactive dashboard organized around decision questions materialize the tool. The research contributes a reproducible analytical instrument for digital political marketing that goes beyond superficial engagement metrics by qualifying them through sentiment and organizing them in an actionable way.

\noindent
\textbf{Keywords}: digital political marketing; sentiment analysis; topic modeling; engagement (COBRAs); growth; data science.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
